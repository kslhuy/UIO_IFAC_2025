### **Notation** (based on input variables and internal calculations):

Let:

* $d_k$: current distance between `host` and `target` (i.e., $d(1)$)
* $\text{buffer} = [d_k, d_{k-1}, \dots, d_{k-N+1}]$: distance buffer of length $N = \text{self.buffer_size}$
* $T_s$: sampling time
* $a_{\text{host}}$: acceleration of the host
* $a_y$: measured or estimated acceleration of the target
* $\text{ID}_{\text{host}}$, $\text{ID}_{\text{target}}$: indices of host and target vehicles
* $d_{\text{norm}} = 17$: normalization constant
* $\text{is\_nearby}$: binary flag (true or false)
* $\omega_{\text{nearby}}, \omega$: exponents for scoring depending on proximity (i.e., `self.wa_nearby` and `self.wa`)

### **Step-by-Step Mathematical Equations**

#### 1. **Compute relative velocity estimate**

If the buffer is fully initialized:

$$
v_{\text{rel}} = \frac{d_{k-N+1} - d_k}{(N - 1) T_s}
$$

$$
v_{\text{rel}}^{-1} = \frac{d_{k-N+2} - d_{k-1}}{(N - 1) T_s}
$$

#### 2. **Estimate relative acceleration**

$$
a_{\text{rel,expected}} = \frac{v_{\text{rel}} - v_{\text{rel}}^{-1}}{(N - 1) T_s}
$$

If buffer is not filled, then approximate:

$$
v_{\text{rel}} = \frac{d_2 - d_1}{T_s}, \quad a_{\text{rel,expected}} = 0
$$

---

#### 3. **Compute actual relative acceleration**

$$
a_{\text{rel,actual}} = a_y - a_{\text{host}}
$$

---

#### 4. **Compute difference in relative acceleration**

$$
\Delta a = a_{\text{rel,expected}} - a_{\text{rel,actual}}
$$

---

#### 5. **Define regulation constant $C$ and distance adjustment $d_{\text{add}}$:**

* If not nearby:

$$
d_{\text{add}} = \frac{d_{\text{norm}} \cdot |\text{ID}_{\text{target}} - \text{ID}_{\text{host}}|}{d_k}
$$

* If nearby:

  * If $\text{ID}_{\text{target}} > 1$:

    * Let:

      $$
      \text{SignMismatch} = \begin{cases}
      0 & \text{if } \text{sign}(a_{\text{host}}) = \text{sign}(a_{\text{rel,actual}}) \\
      1 & \text{otherwise}
      \end{cases}
      $$
    * Then:

      $$
      C = \begin{cases}
      0.8 & \text{if SignMismatch} = 0 \\
      0.85 & \text{if SignMismatch} = 1
      \end{cases}
      $$

    $$
    d_{\text{add}} = \frac{d_{\text{norm}} \cdot C}{d_k}
    $$

  * Else (i.e., $\text{ID}_{\text{target}} \leq 1$):

    $$
    d_{\text{add}} = \frac{d_{\text{norm}} \cdot C}{d_k} \quad \text{(with default } C = 1 \text{)}
    $$

---

#### 6. **Compute score**

$$
a_{\text{score}} = \max \left(1 - \left| d_{\text{add}} \cdot \Delta a \right|, \; 0 \right)
$$

---

#### 7. **Apply proximity-based weighting**

$$
a_{\text{score}} =
\begin{cases}
\left(a_{\text{score}}\right)^{\omega_{\text{nearby}}} & \text{if is\_nearby} = \text{True} \\
\left(a_{\text{score}}\right)^{\omega} & \text{if is\_nearby} = \text{False}
\end{cases}
$$

---

### ✅ Final Formula Summary:

$$
a_{\text{score}} = \left[ \max \left(1 - \left| \frac{d_{\text{scale}}}{d_k} \cdot \left( \frac{v_{\text{rel}} - v_{\text{rel}}^{-1}}{(N - 1) T_s} - (a_y - a_{\text{host}}) \right) \right|, 0 \right) \right]^{\omega_{\text{nearby/normal}}}
$$

Where:

* $d_{\text{scale}} = d_{\text{norm}} \cdot C$
* $\omega_{\text{nearby/normal}} = \omega_{\text{nearby}}$ if `is_nearby` else $\omega$
